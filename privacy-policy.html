Last updated: July 2026

RJ Holidays ("we", "our", "us") operates rjholidays.online. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you share with us when you enquire about or book a tour package.

How We Use Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share necessary details with hotels, transport providers, and local guides solely to fulfil your booked trip.

Data Security
