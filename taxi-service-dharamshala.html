Where We Go

Popular Routes & Coverage

From quick local hops to multi-day outstation circuits, here's where our taxis run most often.

Local

Dharamshala & McLeodganj Sightseeing

Half-day or full-day local sightseeing covering McLeodganj, Bhagsu Waterfall, Naddi View Point, Dharamkot, Kangra Fort and the Dalai Lama Temple Complex, with waiting time at each stop.

Transfer

Airport & Railway Station Transfers

Pickup/drop at Gaggal (Dharamshala) Airport (DHM), Pathankot Railway Station and Amritsar Airport, with flight/train tracking so you're never kept waiting.

Outstation

Dharamshala → Dalhousie & Khajjiar

A scenic 3-3.5 hour drive to the misty colonial hill station of Dalhousie and the meadows of Khajjiar — popular as a day trip or overnight run.

Outstation

Dharamshala → Manali

Approximately 7-8 hours via Mandi. Available as a one-way drop or a multi-day driver-on-hire package if you're continuing on to Solang Valley or Rohtang Pass.

Outstation

Dharamshala → Amritsar (Golden Temple)

A popular same-day or overnight run (approx. 4-4.5 hours) for pilgrims and travellers combining the Golden Temple and Wagah Border with their Himachal trip.

Outstation

Dharamshala → Chandigarh / Delhi

Long-distance drops for onward flight or train connections, available as a private one-way cab with a fixed, pre-quoted fare.
